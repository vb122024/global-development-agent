# Third-party sources and attribution

## World Bank indicators

The dashboard's country indicators are derived from the World Bank's World Development Indicators and retrieved through the World Bank Indicators API. Attribute the World Bank when reusing the data, retain source links and dates, and identify material changes to the data. The World Bank's data-access terms and the terms for the specific dataset apply to reuse.

## World Bank reports

The project links to selected World Bank reports for retrieval and citation. The source catalogue records the work-level licence for each reviewed report. Those reports are not licensed by this repository's MIT License; retain the report's own attribution and licence when reusing it.

## Map materials

The India outline is sourced from Survey of India material. Its terms apply independently of this repository's licence. Retain the required attribution and review the current Survey of India terms before reuse, redistribution or commercial use.

## No endorsement

Global Development Intelligence is an independent mini experiment. The World Bank, Survey of India and other source providers do not sponsor, endorse or approve this project.
